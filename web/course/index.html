Life insurance

Explore life insurance products, the business environment, customer responsibilities, capital management, financial reporting, and shareholder value.

  1. 1. Protection and savings products

    Product categories, term assurance, income protection, critical illness, endowments, universal life and investment bonds.

  2. 2. Retirement income and other products

    Pensions, annuities, drawdown, wraps, variable annuities, equity release, takaful and microinsurance.

  3. 3. Life insurance product bases

    With-profits business, methods of distributing surplus, unit-linked and index-linked business.

  4. 4. The business environment

    Regulation, demand, competition, technology, emerging risks, distribution, outsourcing and corporate finance.

  5. 5. Legislation and customer protection

    Fair contracts, discrimination, personal data, security and the responsibilities associated with data science.

  6. 6. Taxation principles

    Government objectives, policyholder taxation, trading profits, the I-E basis and excess expenses.

  7. 7. Taxation in practice

    UK life insurance taxation, deferred tax and comparisons with other jurisdictions.

  8. 8. The regulatory environment

    Regulatory objectives, supervision, actuarial roles, business transfers and international approaches.

  9. 9. Solvency frameworks and governance

    Solvency II governance, own risk and solvency assessments, reporting, groups and international comparisons.

  10. 10. Solvency valuation and capital

    Asset and liability valuation, the risk margin, capital calculations, diversification and the quality of capital.

  11. 13. Capital management

    Capital needs, available capital, solvency projections, proxy models and methods of managing the capital position.

  12. 14. Asset–liability management

    Investment principles, matching different product types, stochastic projections and the use of derivatives.

  13. 15. Analysis of surplus

    Sources of surplus, experience and assumption changes, roll-forward calculations and management decisions.

  14. 16. Profit reporting

    Financial statements, accounting principles, IFRS 9, IFRS 17 measurement and profit emergence, and US GAAP.

  15. 17. Embedded value

    Embedded value principles, capital costs, links to solvency, analysis of change and customer value.
