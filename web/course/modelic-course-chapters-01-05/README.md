# ModelIC course — first five pages

These are plain Markdown course drafts adapted from your chapter notes. Your original notes have not been changed.

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| [1. Protection and savings products](01-protection-and-savings-products.md) | Product categories, term assurance, income protection, critical illness, endowments, universal life and investment bonds. |
| [2. Retirement income and other products](02-retirement-income-and-other-products.md) | Individual and group pensions, annuities, income drawdown, wraps, variable annuities, equity release, takaful and microinsurance. |
| [3. Life insurance product bases](03-life-insurance-product-bases.md) | With-profits business and methods of distributing surplus, unit-linked business and index-linked business. |
| [4. The business environment](04-the-business-environment.md) | Regulation, demand, competition, technology, emerging risks, distribution, outsourcing and corporate finance. |
| [5. Legislation and customer protection](05-legislation-and-customer-protection.md) | Fair contracts, discrimination, personal data, security and the responsibilities associated with data science. |

The original notes combine chapters 1 and 2. The division between these two course pages is editorial: page 2 begins at individual personal pensions. It does not claim to reproduce the original textbook’s chapter boundary.

Most of the detail and wording has been retained. Explanatory bullets have been joined into paragraphs, genuine lists of alternatives have been kept, and product sections generally place the customer’s perspective before the insurer’s perspective. Short introductions and additional subheadings provide a reading sequence.

The drafts do not add ModelIC exercises or claim that the model already implements the products discussed. Those can be designed around the course content later.

[Editorial notes](EDITORIAL_NOTES.md) record the substantive clarifications, omissions and remaining review points. They are for you, rather than part of the course text.
